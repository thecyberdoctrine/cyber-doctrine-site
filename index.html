How long does the 30-day pilot take to implement?

The pilot can be launched in under 24 hours. Employees receive access immediately, and training plus simulations run throughout the 30 days.

Do we need IT involvement?

No. The pilot is fully managed by us and requires minimal setup. We handle training delivery, phishing simulations, and reporting.

What happens after the 30-day pilot?

You receive a risk report, employee performance insights, and recommended next steps. You can continue with a monthly program when it is available or stop with no obligation.
